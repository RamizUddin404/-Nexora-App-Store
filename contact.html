Contact NEXORA

NEXORA is an independent app store and digital tools showcase created and maintained by Ramiz Uddin. The platform brings together published Android apps, practical browser utilities, Termux resources, and software projects in one place.

About the creator

Name: Ramiz Uddin

Role: Creator, owner and administrator of NEXORA

What NEXORA offers

  • Browse available apps and software projects
  • Download published APK releases when available
  • Use built-in browser utilities
  • Find project descriptions and installation guidance

Ownership & publishing

Only authorized NEXORA administrators manage app listings, releases, descriptions, and published files. Visitors can view and download available content; public project submissions and uploads are not supported.
